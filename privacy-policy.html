IntraAI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

In short
  • The AI runs on your phone. Your chats, voice and images are processed on the device and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app uses the internet only to download AI models and to handle subscriptions through Google Play.

Your chats

Your messages and the AI's answers are created on your phone and stored only on your phone. A chat is deleted automatically one week after you last used it, and you can delete any chat yourself at any time (long-press it in Recents). Uninstalling the app deletes all chats.

Voice mode

Voice mode uses your microphone, only while voice mode is open. Speech is turned into text on your phone; audio is never recorded to a file or sent anywhere. Answers are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

Image creation

Images are created on your phone. They are saved to your Pictures folder only when you tap Save.

Downloads

When you download an AI model, the app fetches it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co); the voice recognition model is downloaded from alphacephei.com. Like any website, these services see your IP address when you download. No chat content or personal information is sent in these downloads.

Subscriptions

IntraAI Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives whether your subscription is active. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Reporting answers or images

If you report an AI answer or image, the app opens your email app with a prepared report containing the reported text (or image prompt), the model name and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use reports only to improve the app's safety.

Data we collect

None. We don't run servers that receive your data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. We don't sell or share any data.

Children

IntraAI is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
